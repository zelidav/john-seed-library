Rarity rating — how it works

Rarity answers one question: how hard is this pack to acquire if you decided you wanted one today? It's a 1–5 opinionated score that combines breeder reputation, the type of seeds in the pack, sealed/opened status, and any strain-specific hype.

It's not the same as availability or value:

  • Rarity = how scarce the pack is in collectors' hands (long-term).
  • Availability = is the breeder/retailer selling it right now (short-term, see the section below).
  • Value = estimated dollar amount to acquire one (currently hidden in this view).

A strain can be high-rarity but in-stock (an Ethos hype drop just hit the seedbank), or low-rarity but sold out (a commercial line that got discontinued).

The five tiers

How a score is calculated (each card has its own breakdown — click to see)

  1. Breeder base tier (1–5) — the dominant input. Top-tier breeders (A&B Company sealed boutique, Ethos Bx projects, Cult Classics hype line, Cookies Fam S1 releases, Tiki Madman post-Cup winners) start at 4–5. Established craft breeders (Exotic Genetix, Mephisto, Bloom, Envy) start at 3. Smaller craft (Geist, Crane City, Fire Farms, Humble Jungle) start at 2. Unsigned starts at 1.
  2. Strain-specific hype override (+0 to +3) — some named drops outrank their breeder's normal tier. In this library: Gushers S1 (5), Cement Shoes S1 (5), Ethos OGDLUX Bx3 (5), Cult Classic Fresh Baked (5), A&B's Jurassic Kush pendant box (5), Dinosaur Biscuits sealed box (5), Drive Thru Wedding (4), Hitmaker (4), Sunset Freshies (4), Freshmaker (4), Grandmas Pajamas (4).
  3. Sealed status (+1, max 5) — unopened packs preserve authentication (hologram, hand-label, breeder seal) and command premium on aftermarket.
  4. Seed type modifier — S1 self-pollinated and Bx backcross releases trade at a collector premium vs. regular fem; reflected in both the per-strain hype list and pricing.
  5. Tester / freebie adjustment — Tester packs were never retailed (forced to ≥4 because they're impossible to buy). Freebies are bonus packs that came with other orders and have reduced standalone value (-1).

The score is clamped to 1–5. Each strain card shows its specific breakdown when you click into it — you'll see exactly which factors moved it up or down from the base.

Availability — current market status

Availability answers a different question than rarity: can you buy this today? A popular craft strain (high-rarity) may still be in stock if the breeder just did a drop. Conversely, a commercial line (low-rarity) may be discontinued.

Click any strain card to read a specific availability note — where it's sold today, aftermarket status, and distribution channel.
