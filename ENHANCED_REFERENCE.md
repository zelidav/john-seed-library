# John Seed Library — Enhanced Genetics Reference

Enhanced from `Strain_Genetics_Reference.docx` (April 2026) using the 42 pack / inventory photos in `John Seed Library` Drive folder.

## What changed vs. the docx

- **9 strains added** that appear on the handwritten inventory cards but were not in the docx:
  - *Sealed:* Cement Shoes S1, Freshmaker, Cloud Spin, S. Orange Pucker, O. Cookie Violence, N. Speckley Boogie
  - *Plus 10 "bonus" strains visible in pack photos but not on any inventory card* (Grapaya Drip, Sin City Blue Zu, Titanium Jack × B-Side, Papaya F2, The Hydra, Dinosaur Biscuits, Copper Sunset, Kali's Cookies, The A Frame, plus the unidentified Tiki Madman pack).
- **Genetics confirmed / corrected from photos:**
  - *Cult Classic Fresh Baked* — confirmed **Purple Goji OG × Freshies** (docx listed pairing as unconfirmed).
  - *Sunset Freshies* — confirmed **Sunset Strip #3 × Freshies** (docx guessed Sunset Sherbet).
  - *G. Classics Hitmaker* — confirmed **Motorbreath × Freshies** (motor breath lineage validated).
  - *GSRH Yahemi* — pack label shows **Melonatta × Project 4516**, not the GSRH/Blue Sherbet base the docx speculated. This is a distinct cross despite the naming overlap.
  - *Exotic Greezy Runtz* — confirmed **Grease Monkey reversal × Runtz** (not a "Greezo" pheno).
  - *Jurassic Kush* — confirmed **Platinum Kush Mints × Stankasaurus** via A&B Company pack (docx guessed Hindu Kush / OG Kush ancestry).
  - *Wilmeac* — breeder confirmed as **Envy Genetics**, lineage **Fruity Pebbles OG × Calisunset** (docx had it as unknown craft).
  - *Animal Kush* — pack says **CBD** (Humble Jungle Seeds), not CBP as docx transcribed.
  - *Crane City S. Socker* — pack in hand is **"The Zit"** (Zkittlez × undisclosed) by Crane City — same breeder, possibly a rename / sibling cross.
  - *Bloom Guava Barz* — pack shows **12** seeds (docx listed 4). Genetics confirmed Biscotti × Guavaz 74.
  - *Nephisto MBAP* — pack shows **~7** seeds (docx listed 5).
- **Ethos OGDLUX** — pack confirms **Bx3** backcross generation (not in docx).
